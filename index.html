The dinner card

À deux, chez Jeanty

Designed for one very specific appetite: chicken, eggs, and everything lovely around them.

First course

Crème de Tomate en Croûte

Tomato soup baked under puff pastry—a warm, theatrical French-bistro beginning.

Ask: no bacon, lard, gelatin, or meat stock.
Main course

Poularde aux Morilles

Chicken breast with morel mushrooms and Camembert sauce. The dish to try if it is available tonight.

Confirm the cooking fat and stock.
For the table

Gratin or greens

Share the Gratin Dauphinois or haricots verts—both pair beautifully with the creamy morel and Camembert chicken.

Our quiet rule: skip the Coq au Vin unless the kitchen can remove the smoked bacon and confirm the sauce base. Also avoid the Lyonnaise salad and onion soup unless the team confirms their bacon/beef-stock ingredients. Menus change, so we’ll ask kindly when ordering.
